# NexusGuard Technologies Ltd. | AUP Quick Reference Card | GDL-AUP-001

## ACCEPTABLE USE QUICK REFERENCE

_Think before you click, send, install or share._

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| **Use company devices** and approved apps for work | **Forward work email or files** to personal accounts such as Gmail |
| **Lock your screen** when you step away (auto-lock at 10 minutes) | **Install unapproved software** or switch off security tools |
| **Use MFA** and a unique password of 12+ characters | **Mine cryptocurrency** or run personal workloads on company resources |
| **Send sensitive data** only through approved, encrypted channels | **Store company data** on personal cloud, USB drives or chat apps |
| **Use the VPN** on public Wi-Fi and keep your home router updated | **Access or share** illegal or offensive content |
| **Ask first** before using a new app, cloud service or AI tool | **Post confidential, client** or incident details on social media |

**Personal devices (BYOD)**

- Enroll the device before using it for work
- Work data stays in the managed container
- No PHI or card data on personal devices

**Working remotely**

- VPN with MFA, always
- WPA2/WPA3 Wi-Fi and a changed router password
- Keep screens and calls private; no printing sensitive files at home

**Cloud and AI tools**

- Approved services only, with company accounts
- No sensitive or client data in unapproved AI tools
- No public or anonymous share links

## YOU ARE MONITORED

NexusGuard monitors email, web, network, device and cloud activity on its systems for security and compliance. There is no expectation of privacy on company systems.

## REPORT WITHIN 1 HOUR

Lost device, phishing, misdirected email or suspected misuse: tell the Service Desk or Security Operations through the incident reporting channel. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation, and prompt reporting counts in your favor.

## CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATIONS

| Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal warning and retraining | Written warning, access removed | Final warning or suspension | Dismissal and legal action |

**Sending client data to a personal account is gross misconduct.**
