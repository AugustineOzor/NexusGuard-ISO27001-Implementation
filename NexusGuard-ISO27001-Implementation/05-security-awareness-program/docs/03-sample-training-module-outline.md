## Knowledge check

1. An email says your password expires in 2 hours and offers a "Keep my password" link. What do you do? A. Click quickly before it expires. B. Reply to ask if it is real. **C. Go to the portal yourself and report it.** D. Forward it to your colleagues.
2. Which is the strongest sign that an email may be phishing? A. It has a company logo. **B. Look-alike sender and an urgent tone.** C. It arrived at 9 am. D. It has an email signature.
3. You typed your password into a page that looked like the login. What do you do first? A. Wait and see what happens. B. Change the password and tell nobody. **C. Report it now and reset the password.** D. Delete the email.
4. A caller says they are a client and asks you to read out personal details to "verify" them. What do you do? A. Give the details, they are a client. **B. Call back on a known number; share the minimum.** C. Ask them to email instead. D. Hang up and say nothing.
5. You get an MFA approval request you did not start. What do you do? A. Approve it to stop the alerts. B. Ignore it. **C. Deny it and report it.** D. Approve it and reset your password later.

_Interactive elements: the two exercises use pause prompts and slide links that jump to the answer or debrief slides._
